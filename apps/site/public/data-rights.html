Data Rights

How to ask for a copy of your qalm data, correct it, export it, or delete your account.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

01Requests you can make02How to make a request03Parents, guardians, and schools04What deletion covers05Response timing06If you are not satisfied
01

Requests you can make

  • Access: get a copy of the personal information qalm holds about you.
  • Correction: fix inaccurate account information.
  • Export: receive your account records and notebooks in a structured, machine-readable format. You can also export any notebook yourself, at any time, as a PDF or a full .qalm backup.
  • Deletion: delete your account and everything in it.
  • Objection and restriction: object to processing based on our legitimate interests, or ask us to pause processing while a concern is resolved.
  • Withdraw permission: turn AI access off for a notebook, or disconnect an agent in Settings, at any time.
02

How to make a request

Email privacy@apollolearn.app from the email address you sign in to qalm with, and say which request you are making. To protect your account, we act only on requests we can verify came from the account owner or someone authorised to act for them, and we may ask for more information to confirm this. Making a request is free.

03

Parents, guardians, and schools

A parent, guardian, or school can make a request about a young person’s account. We will ask for reasonable proof of authority before releasing, changing, or deleting their data, and may involve them where that is appropriate for their age.

04

What deletion covers

qalm does not have a delete-account button yet. To delete your account, email privacy@apollolearn.app as described above. We then delete your account and sign-in sessions, your notebooks with their pages, ink, text, comments and readings, the files you imported, and your AI access settings, agent approvals and activity log, and we disconnect any agents. We keep only a non-identifying record that a deletion was completed. Deleted data leaves our backups as they roll over.

  • Copies on your devices stay until you sign out, clear the site’s data, or uninstall the app, because we cannot reach your devices to erase them.
  • Deleting a notebook forever in the app removes its pages and ink from our servers straight away; see the Privacy Notice for the small record we keep.
  • Anything an AI agent already received is held by your AI provider. Ask them to delete it under their own terms.
05

Response timing

We aim to respond within one month of receiving a verified request. If a request is complex or we receive many requests, we may take up to two further months and will tell you why. If we cannot fulfil a request, we will explain the reason.

06

If you are not satisfied

Reply to our response and we will review it again. If you live in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk), and in the EU or EEA to your local data protection authority.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: privacy@apollolearn.app. Account help and anything else: support@apollolearn.app.
