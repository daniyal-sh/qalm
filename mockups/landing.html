03AI, if you want it

It reads along. It never touches your ink.

Connect your own AI agent, like Claude, over MCP. While you write, it sees only what changed, reads your handwriting itself, and answers with comments pinned beside your ink.

1

You write, then pause

qalm waits for a pause in your writing, or for you to tap Ask.

2

Your agent gets a small crop

R7 [1] p3 @96,712 486x88 +9 -0

New ink in black, the ink around it in grey. Not the whole page.

3

It comments beside your ink

CClaudeC1
Looks right. To check, differentiate: x ln x + x/2 − x/2 = x ln x.

Pinned to those strokes, it follows them if you move them. Reply, and it answers in the thread.

The ground rules

  • It reads only the notebooks you share. AI access is off until you turn it on, one notebook at a time.
  • It connects only after you approve it, and you can pause or stop it any time.
  • It leaves comments pinned to your ink, and can save its reading of your handwriting if you allow it.
  • It can never change, move or erase your ink.

Light on tokens

Your agent gets a few small crops of what changed, never screenshots of whole pages. Once it has read something, it can save that reading as text and skip the picture next time.

There's no AI running on our side. It's your agent, on your own account, and it reads your handwriting with its own eyes.
