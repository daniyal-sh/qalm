AI in qalm

Your own AI, following your handwriting live.

qalm has no AI of its own. You connect an agent you already use, such as Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client, and it runs wherever it runs. While you write, it receives compact updates: a small picture of each changed line plus your device's guess at the text. It answers with comments next to your lines. It can read and comment, but it can never touch your ink.

MCP only, no server-side AI Read and comment, never edit ink About 10× cheaper than screenshots Off by default, per notebook

The big picture

There are three parts: your device, qalm's server, and your agent. The server syncs your notes and translates ink into something an agent can read cheaply. Any thinking happens in your agent, not in qalm.

Readings Building now

Select a line and tap "Reading" to see the saved text: your device's guess or the agent's transcription. Edit it to fix it. Your edit is marked as yours, and agents can never overwrite it; they're told to leave a comment instead.

What the agent actually receives

Not screenshots of whole pages. Each update is a few lines of plain text ("Q-ink Live"), plus one small image strip with only the lines that changed. That's why it's accurate and cheap.

# one update, after a 6 s pause #q s=calc2 r=431 p3 "Q3 ∫x^2 ln x dx" L3 v=x^3/3 +L4 [1] ~"=x3/3 lnx - ∫x3/3 1/x dx" +L5 [2] ~"=x3/3lnx-x3/9" -L6 re C1 "why x²/3 and not x³/3?" note C7 p3:L5 "same as the book?" ask "is L5 right?"

The image is rendered on the server from your strokes, in black on white with row numbers, so the agent can read handwriting the on-device guess got wrong (like "x3" that is really x³).

Updates are self-contained, so the agent's app can trim old ones. Coming When an agent first opens a page it gets only the open threads, each with its latest message, not the whole history.

The four tools

That's the agent's entire vocabulary. There's no "tutor mode": the protocol is general, and how the agent behaves (tutor, study buddy, note-taker) is up to you and your agent's instructions.

ToolArgumentsWhat it doesExample
waits?Waits for the next update (long-poll). Without s, lists the live sessions it can see.wait(s:"calc2") → the update above
readrefReads anything on demand, as text plus an image strip where needed.read("p3:L2-9"), read("outline"), read("pdf:p2")
transcriberef, textSaves its reading of a line, so later updates send text instead of an image. Refused if you wrote that line's text yourself.transcribe("p3:L5", "x^3/3 ln x - x^3/9")
commentref or thread, text, resolve?Starts a thread next to a line, or replies in one. Never changes ink.comment(thread:"C1", "Because du = …")

You're in control

Every rule is enforced on the server on every call, not just hidden in the app.

Ink is read-only

The server has no way for an agent to add, move or erase ink. Agents can only add comments and, if allowed, readings.

Per notebook, off by default

An agent sees a notebook only if you granted it and its AI access is on. Trashed and deleted notebooks are invisible.

Separate switches

Commenting and saving readings each have their own switch per agent. Your own readings can't be overwritten.

Pause, Stop, Disconnect

Pause sharing from the pill, Stop to turn AI access off, or disconnect an agent everywhere in Settings. All take effect on its next call.

Calm by design

Updates only after a 6 s pause, at most one per 20 s. Asks, comments and replies go at once. Erased scribbles never leave your device's session.

Your agent, your data

qalm runs no AI and stores no agent memory. The agent you chose sees only what you shared, under its own provider's terms.

Why it's cheap

Estimated agent cost for one hour of maths tutoring (about 60 updates), at GPT-6 Luna rates. The first thing to measure with real agents is how each provider counts image tokens.

Full-page screenshots$1.36/h · $0.17 cached
qalm Q-ink Live~$0.13/h · ~$0.025 cached

Peak context: ~219k tokens with screenshots vs ~38k with Q-ink.

Where the savings come from

  • Only changed lines are sent, never the whole page.
  • One small black-on-white strip per update, not a full-resolution screenshot.
  • Lines the agent already transcribed come back as text, with no image.
  • Threads add only your words, a few tokens each.
  • On-device reading is free (ML Kit), and qalm runs no model on its servers.
